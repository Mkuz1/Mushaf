**Full description (max 4000):**
Mus'haf is a clean, distraction-free Quran reader built around the Madinah Mushaf
(Hafs), the 604-page layout known and loved worldwide.

Features
• Authentic Madinah Mushaf pages, page-for-page.
• Listen ayah by ayah with 6 reciters — Abdul Basit (Murattal & Mujawwad), Al-Husary,
  Al-Minshawi, Mishary Alafasy and Salah Bukhatir — the reader follows the recitation.
• Right-to-left page turning, just like a printed Mus'haf.
• Single page on phones, two-page spread on tablets and foldables.
• Night mode — a soft, warm theme that rests the eyes in low light.
• Full index — jump to any Surah or Juz instantly.
• Resume — reopens exactly where you stopped reading.
• Pinch to zoom, tap to hide the controls for fullscreen reading.
• Arabic and English interface, switchable any time.
• Works offline — pages are saved as you read; download all for full offline use.
• Lightweight install — pages download on demand to keep the app small.

Mus'haf was created as a Sadaqah Jariyah. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

May Allah accept it from all who contributed.
