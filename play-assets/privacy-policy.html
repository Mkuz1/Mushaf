Privacy Policy — Mus'haf

Last updated: 2026

Mus'haf ("the app") is a Quran reader provided as a Sadaqah Jariyah. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences locally on your device only (your last-read page, language choice, and light/dark setting), and caches Quran page images so they are available offline. This information never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Network use

To display pages, the app downloads Quran page images from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), with a third-party fallback host (files.quran.app). Ayah text, translations and tafsir come from api.alquran.cloud, api.quran.com and quranenc.com. When you listen to a recitation, the audio is downloaded from everyayah.com, and Google Play is asked whether an app update is available. These are ordinary requests and contain no personal data about you. We do not control those hosts' logging; no identifying information is sent by the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
