Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Mushaf is a Sadaqa Jariyah: no price, no ads, no subscription and no account, on every platform.

Which mushaf is it?

The Madinah Mushaf printed by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, Hafs narration, all 604 pages as printed.

Does it work offline?

Pages you have opened stay on your device. Download all 604 once, about 57 MB, and the whole mushaf works without a connection.

Can I memorise with it?

Set a range of verses and repeat each verse or the whole range as many times as you need, up to ∞, with any of the 26 reciters.

Does it collect my data?

No. Bookmarks, notes and settings stay on your device. The app only downloads pages, texts and recitations from public sources.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is in English, French and Arabic, with translations and tafsir in all three.

Does it work on a TV?

Yes, on Google TV, Android TV and Apple TV, with everything usable from the remote. Get the Apple TV app from the App Store.

Can I move my notes to another device?

Export your notes and bookmarks as a file from the settings and import them on any other device running Mushaf.
